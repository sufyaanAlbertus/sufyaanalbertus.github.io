The pitch

The Hive is starving and you're its last scout. Race across a glowing meadow, load up on pollen and make it home before the sun goes down. Every delivery calls another worker bee to your side, and your swarm becomes your shield against the wasps closing in. Fly further for bigger rewards, but the meadow fights back.

The challenge

One weekend. One theme: Hive Mind. My answer: start alone and grow into a living swarm, one mind flying many bodies.

What I did

Designed and built the whole experience: the core loop, the C++ gameplay, the honey-gold UI, the music and every sound effect. Every model, image and sound is generated in code. Zero store-bought assets.
