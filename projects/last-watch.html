The pitch

You're not the captain. You're the crewmate left behind to guard a mountain of treasure you'll never get to spend. As the sun beats down, skeletons claw their way out of the sand in endless waves. Fight, scavenge and survive as long as you can, because nobody is coming back for you.

The challenge

Take a one-page concept to a playable survival game with a strong hook, a tight loop and a look you'd recognise from one screenshot.

What I did

Wrote the concept, designed the fight-scavenge-endure loop, set the toy-box art direction and built the combat, waves, pickups and HUD.
